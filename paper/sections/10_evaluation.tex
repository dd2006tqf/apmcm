The table separates two things. The new-inundation area moves with the terrain and probability thresholds, which is what the sensitivity surface of Fig.~\ref{fig:q3_threshold_surface} is for. The decisions taken from it do not: the city grade never leaves ``general'', the unit ranking keeps a Spearman correlation of at least 0.889 under every alternative input of 16 July, and the relief plan changes only when the flood extent on the roads changes. The largest single change in the ranking (Spearman 0.662) comes from using the 4 July instead of the 16 July image, i.e. from new information, not from a modeling choice.

\subsection{Strengths}

Validation is spatially blocked with a buffer, every tuning step uses a separate calibration fold, and the leave-one-cluster-out test leaves IoU 0.508, so the numbers reported here are held-out estimates. The same raster $F$ defines the hazard of Problem~4 and the cut network of Problem~5, the Problem~4 population and index set demand and priority, and the solver returns status optimal with zero gap on every run. At town-centre resolution, relief is limited by isolation, and that holds under every assumption tested.

\subsection{Innovations}

Two modeling choices were settled before training. First, the labels were read before the target was chosen: on all 65.5\,M pixels they mark new inundation, so the recognizer learns $F$ directly while Problem~3 still enforces $F\cap W_0=\emptyset$ with an independent $W_0$, which removes a systematic over-count of permanent water and keeps the training target identical to the problem's definition. Second, the area is the calibration target, not the 0.5 or maximal F1 threshold that such models usually use: probabilities are calibrated isotonically and the threshold is chosen so that the mask area equals the calibrated expected area \eqref{eq:tauA}, with conformal intervals per tile. Total area error of M6 falls from $+4.1\%$ to $+1.45\%$ without touching the network, maps and reported areas stay identical, and the uncalibrated M2 reaches $+0.9\%$ after the 50 px MMU but has no conformal interval.



Two further choices concern how a result is read. Grades are separated where adjacent components of a Gaussian mixture of the logit flood share are equally likely \eqref{eq:gmm_bayes}, the thresholds carry bootstrap intervals, and each unit receives a probability vector \eqref{eq:grade_mc}, so the grading is reproducible from data and unreliable grades advertise themselves. The flood map then enters the relief model twice: the same raster removes roads and depots and generates demand and priority, so the worth of the next satellite pass becomes the change of the optimal unmet share (3.9 points), a number an emergency manager can weigh against the cost of extra acquisitions.



\subsection{Conclusions}

We built one chain from images to relief. On the official data, a LightGBM pixel classifier reaches IoU 0.618 with a total area error of $-0.2\%$ after a 50 px MMU; a dual-branch gated SAR--optical U-Net reaches IoU 0.797 and $+0.9\%$ in-domain after the same MMU, while its calibrated modality-dropout variant used for transfer reaches IoU 0.730 and $+1.45\%$. Four intensity grades are separated at flood shares of 5.9\%, 53.0\% and 96.9\%. Transferred to Hengzhou with co-registered Sentinel-1 data and physical guards, the model finds 26.90\,km$^2$ of new inundation on 16 July (0.78\% of the city, grade ``general'') and a net change of $-52.53$\,km$^2$ caused by water receding after a wet baseline. On 393 settlement catchments the assessment ranks Shitang, Maling and Luancheng as the most affected zones, with a ranking that is stable under weight perturbation. The relief MILP opens six depots and serves 7316 of 13,948 people in need; the remaining need is cut off by water, not short of supplies, which defines where the authority should send boats rather than trucks. That share equals the weighted demand of the isolated units, so it is a floor and not slack; 600 scenarios that perturb both the demand and the roads put its CVaR$_{95}$ at 0.651 and never make the plan infeasible\cite{deepseek2026,qwen2026,doubao2026}.
