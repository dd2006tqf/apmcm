\subsection{Strengths and Innovations}

Validation is spatially blocked with a buffer, every tuning step uses a separate calibration fold, and leave-one-cluster-out leaves IoU 0.508, so every number here is a held-out estimate. One raster $F$ supplies the hazard, the cut network, demand and priority, and the MILP returns optimal with zero gap on every run. At town-centre resolution relief is limited by isolation, and that holds under every assumption tested.

Every choice that could have peeked at the test tiles was made on the calibration fold. The label audit came first: all 65.5\,M valid pixels mark new inundation, not post-flood water. The network learns $F$ directly while Problem~3 subtracts an independent $W_0$, so permanent water never enters the target. Segmentation usually cuts at 0.5 or the F1 optimum; here the mask area matches the calibrated expected area \eqref{eq:tauA}, with a conformal interval on every tile. M6 closes at $+1.45\%$ against $+4.1\%$ uncalibrated, network untouched; uncalibrated M2+MMU reaches $+0.9\%$ but states no interval. Grades sit on mixture boundaries with bootstrap intervals, so a grade is a probability vector \eqref{eq:grade_mc}. The raster enters relief twice, which makes the next satellite pass worth 3.9 points of unmet share.

\subsection{Conclusions}

One chain runs from images to relief. LightGBM reaches IoU 0.618 at $-0.2\%$ after the 50\,px MMU; the gated U-Net reaches 0.797 at $+0.9\%$ in-domain and 0.730 at $+1.45\%$ after transfer. Grades split at 5.9\%, 53.0\% and 96.9\%. In Hengzhou 16 July gives 26.90\,km$^2$ new, 0.78\% of the city and grade ``general'', with a net $-52.53$\,km$^2$ from receding water. On 393 catchments Shitang, Maling and Luancheng rank highest, stable under weight perturbation. The MILP opens six depots and serves 7316 of 13,948; the rest is cut off by water, so the shortfall is a floor, not slack. Across 600 scenarios CVaR$_{95}$ is 0.651 and the plan stays feasible\cite{deepseek2026,qwen2026,doubao2026}.
